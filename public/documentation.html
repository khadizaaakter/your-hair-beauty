1. Login & Access

Access your admin panel securely from any device.

How to Log In

  • Go to https://yourhairbeauty.co.uk/login
  • Enter your Admin Email Access.
  • Enter your Password.
  • Click Sign In.
Forgot Password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. Enter your email, and you will receive a secure OTP code to reset it immediately.

2. Dashboard Overview

Upon logging in, you will see the Admin Dashboard. This gives you a quick snapshot of your business health.

  • Total Revenue: Real-time sales data.
  • Total Orders: Count of all new orders.
  • Active Customers: Total registered users.
  • Recent Activity: See who just signed up or purchased.

3. Add New Product

Adding products is simple and supports multiple images.

  • Navigate to Products on the sidebar and click "Add Product" (top right).
  • Basic Info: Add Name, Description, and Price.
  • Pricing: Set a "Sale Price" to show a discount badge automatically.
  • Inventory: Enter current stock levels. The system will auto-decrement on sales.
  • Images: Drag & drop multiple images. The first image is the cover photo.
  • Category: Assign a category and subcategory for filters.
  • Click Save Product.

4. Managing Categories

Categories organize your store and create the navigation menu automatically.

Add Main Category

  • Go to Categories in the Admin Panel.
  • Click Add Category.
  • Enter Name (e.g., "Hair Care").
  • Upload a Category Image (displayed on the homepage).
  • Save.

Add Subcategory

To add "Shampoo" under "Hair Care":

  • Find "Hair Care" in the list and click Manage Subcategories (or Edit).
  • Add the subcategory name.
  • It will instantly appear in the shop filters.
Deleting Categories: Validating safety checks prevents deleting categories that contain active products. Reallocate products before deleting a category.

5. Order Management

Track every order from payment to delivery.

Viewing Orders

Go to the Orders page to see a list of all transactions. You can filter by:

  • Pending: Payment received but not yet processed.
  • Processing: Being packed.
  • Shipped: Handed to courier.
  • Delivered: Successful completion.

Processing an Order

  • Click on any order ID to view details.
  • Customer Details: See shipping address and phone number.
  • Items: List of products purchased.
  • Update Status: Change status from "Pending" to "Shipped" when you send the package. The customer can track this status in their account.

6. Store Settings

Customize global variables without coding.

General Settings

  • Store Name & Currency: Set defaults clearly.
  • Free Shipping Threshold: Currently set to £70. Orders above this get free delivery.
  • Delivery Charge: Standard shipping cost (e.g., £3.99).

Banners & Alerts

  • Top Header Text: Update the announcement bar (e.g., "Free Shipping over £70").
  • Hero Slider: Upload prominent visuals for the homepage slideshow.

7. Shop Features (Customer View)

Understanding what your customers see.

  • Category Filters: Customers can filter by Price, Brand, and Subcategory on the shop page.
  • Search: Instant search bar for products.
  • Guest Checkout: Customers don't need an account to buy. They can checkout as guests for speed.
  • Secure Payments: Integrated with Worldpay for secure card processing.
